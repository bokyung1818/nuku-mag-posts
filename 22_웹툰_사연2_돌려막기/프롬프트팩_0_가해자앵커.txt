[공통]
Korean webtoon (manhwa) character reference sheet, single character, clean line art,
soft cel shading, muted realistic palette, plain neutral grey background,
waist-up, facing camera. NO text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere.

[A · 웃는 버전] s2_senior_v1.png
A Korean man in his early forties on a film set. Baseball cap pushed back on his head,
dark khaki field jacket, a two-way radio on his belt, a blank lanyard badge.
He is smiling warmly and openly at someone above his own rank - but his eyes are not
smiling; they stay flat and measuring. Friendly mouth, cold eyes. Ordinary-looking,
not handsome, not a villain caricature. Waist-up, plain grey background.

[B · 굳은 버전] s2_senior_v2.png
The SAME man (use image A as reference), same cap, same jacket, same radio.
Now his face is hard and impatient - jaw set, chin slightly down, looking at someone
below his own rank. Not shouting, not cartoonishly angry: the flat contempt of someone
who knows nothing will happen to him. Waist-up, plain grey background.
→ A를 레퍼런스로 올려서 같은 인물로 유지할 것.
