[bow] 인사 (막내)
He bows politely (90-degree greeting) to a group of busy crew who barely glance at him; awkward first-day mood, warm morning light.

[chores] 잡일 (막내)

[arm] 칭찬 (막내+PD)

[shake] 약속 (막내+PD)
The rookie and the PD shake hands firmly, both smiling as if sealing a promise, on set; rookie grateful, PD confident; warm light.

[night] 탈진 (막내)
Late night, exhausted rookie alone on an empty set coiling heavy cables, dark circles, a convenience-store triangle kimbap and canned coffee on an equipment case; cold blue night light, lonely.

[dir] PD 클로즈업 (PD)
Close-up of the PD (40s) with a warm charming smile but subtly cold, calculating eyes; slightly low angle, backlit softbox glow, foreboding mood.
